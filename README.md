## Audience and purpose

**Audience:** Student athletes who plan their own meals while bulking, cutting or maintaining.

**Purpose:** Help them pick meals from a sample list, see total calories and protein against their own daily targets, and turn the choices into a grocery list.

## Features

1. **Meal picker with live totals (interaction):** Use + and - to choose servings of each sample meal. Calorie and protein totals and progress bars update instantly against targets you enter.
2. **Grocery list generator:** Combines the ingredients of every chosen meal, multiplied by servings, into one checkable list.

## How to use

1. Enter a daily calorie target (1200 to 6000) and protein target (20 to 400 g), then press **Update targets**.
2. Add servings of meals with the + buttons and watch the totals change.
3. Press **Generate grocery list** and tick items off as you shop. Press **Clear all meals** to start over.

## Sources and credits

- All meals, calories, protein values and ingredient amounts are rough, made-up sample numbers for this class project. They are not exact nutrition facts.
- Code drafted with AI assistance (see `AI_USE.md`). No external libraries, images or APIs.

## Known limitations

- Not medical or dietary advice; the app does not recommend targets. Ask a registered dietitian or team staff.
- Only 10 sample meals; you cannot add your own.
- Nothing is saved; refreshing clears your plan.
- Grocery amounts are not rounded to store package sizes.
- Only calories and protein are tracked (no carbs, fat or micronutrients).
